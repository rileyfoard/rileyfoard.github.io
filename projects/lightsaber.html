Caltech Robotics, Fall 2025

ROS2 Robot Lightsaber Duel Simulation

  • ROS2
  • Inverse kinematics
  • Motion planning
  • ME133a

Caltech’s ME133a robotics course covered robot kinematics, inverse kinematics, motion planning, and programming with ROS2. For the final project, a classmate and I decided to apply what we had learned to something more entertaining: a simulated Star Wars lightsaber duel.

Throughout the ten-week course, we worked with multi-degree-of-freedom robot models, coordinate frames, joint-space calculations, and ROS2 simulations. For our project, we combined those tools to plan and animate coordinated robot-arm movements for a lightsaber battle.

The project brought the mathematics of robotics together with an application that was easy to visualize. Instead of solving inverse kinematics as an isolated homework problem, we had to use it to produce motion sequences in a simulated robotic system. It was a fun way to finish a technically demanding course.
